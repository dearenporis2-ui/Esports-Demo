Professionalizing Competitive Gaming in the Seychelles

Building the infrastructure for online competitive gaming — connecting players, fans, and sponsors through a world-class digital platform.

01

Our Mission

To professionalize online competitive gaming in the Seychelles — creating pathways for local talent to compete, grow, and be recognized on a global stage.

02

Our Platform

Built on Battlefy for tournament logistics — with our own branded layer for community, news, and identity. Everything from registration to results, in one place.

03

Our Community

Thousands of players across the islands competing in FC 24, Tekken 8, Valorant and more — a Discord-powered community alive between events.
